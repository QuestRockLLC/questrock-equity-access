Understand the process before you continue.

What is a HELOC?

A home equity line of credit is borrowing secured by your home. Failure to repay could result in loss of your home. Rates, terms, fees, and amounts depend on the product and eligibility.

Who handles my application?

QuestRock is your loan partner and facilitates the transaction. You complete the application, underwriting, closing, and funding with Aven.

Can I keep my existing mortgage?

Qualifying second-lien programs may allow you to retain your first mortgage. Review the available terms before deciding. It is not guaranteed.

How quickly could I receive funds?

Funding may be available in as little as five days. Timing depends on verification, approval, closing, applicable waiting periods, and cleared conditions. It is not guaranteed.

Can I complete the process online?

Many steps are digital. An in-person closing may be required depending on your property state, including Georgia, South Carolina, and Texas.

Will I need to talk to QuestRock?

No. This page does not collect your contact information or schedule a QuestRock callback. Continue with Aven and use Aven’s support if you have questions about the application.
