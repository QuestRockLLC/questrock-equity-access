Common questions

Why am I going to Aven?

QuestRock partners with Aven to provide access to an online home equity application. Aven handles the application, underwriting, closing and funding process.

Why will Aven request personal information?

Aven needs certain information to verify identity, evaluate eligibility and determine available financing options.

Can I keep my current mortgage?

Depending on your circumstances and available financing options, you may be able to access home equity without refinancing your existing first mortgage.

Do I have to speak with a loan officer?

No QuestRock callback is required. You can proceed independently through Aven's application process. Calling QuestRock is optional.
