Who is my loan partner?

QuestRock is your loan partner. We help you get started and stay with you on this HELOC. You continue the application with Aven, who handles underwriting, closing, and funding.

What is a HELOC?

A home equity line of credit is borrowing secured by your home. Failure to repay could result in loss of your home. Rates, terms, fees, and amounts depend on eligibility.

Do I apply with QuestRock?

You start here with QuestRock, your loan partner. There is no QuestRock application form on this page. When you’re ready, continue with Aven to explore your options.

Can I keep my existing mortgage?

A qualifying second-lien HELOC may let you keep your first mortgage. That is not guaranteed. Review the terms before you decide.

How quickly could I receive funds?

Funding may be available in as little as five days. Timing depends on verification, approval, closing, waiting periods, and cleared conditions. It is not guaranteed.

Can I complete the process online?

Many steps are digital. Georgia, South Carolina, and Texas may require an in-person closing.
